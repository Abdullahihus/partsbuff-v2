# Catalog coverage — October 11, 2026

The target was **50 additional sourced OEM records per existing year/model catalog**, usually 20 → 70. Original records were retained with their original references. Additions follow the index's selected source reference. Some catalogs contain more than one engine/body/trim reference: check each record's `vehicleDetails`, source and conditions against your vehicle. These counts do not mean that every listed part fits every VIN for that model.
