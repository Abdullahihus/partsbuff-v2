## Source and fitment

The original 174 records retain their RealOEM links and notes. New records come from [BMWOEMParts.com's 2011 328i catalog](https://www.bmwoemparts.com/v-2011-bmw-328i--base--3-0l-l6-gas/engine--engine-parts). That dealer catalog combines multiple variants. Each addition was checked against an explicit OEM-number/name row and retains its individual diagram, product link and recorded conditions.

Entries explicitly limited to other bodies, N52 engines, all-wheel drive, M3 equipment or earlier production were excluded where identified. Only records with compatible engine/body conditions were retained; ambiguous engine and sedan records were held out. The dealer selection does not prove November 2011/VIN fitment, so **all new records are option-dependent candidates**. Check the original diagram, build date, engine and installed options before ordering.

Direct RealOEM downloads were unavailable. This is not a complete factory EPC export. No source challenge was bypassed. No guessed numbers, live prices, scraping scripts or caches are included in the app.
